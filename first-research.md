## 6. Payment and Iran-specific safeguards

A crypto checkout does not make an otherwise restricted purchase or end use permissible. OpenAI warns that accessing or offering access from unsupported regions can lead to suspension; Anthropic’s supported-regions policy also restricts unsupported-region use. A reseller’s willingness to take USDT is not an exception to those policies.[[help.openai](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9131992-chatgpt-and-api-services-in-unsupported-countries-and-territories)][[anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/supported-regions)]

For the wallet side, use the exact token and network generated by the live checkout. Do not assume that “USDT accepted” means TRC20, ERC20 and BEP20 are interchangeable: AIPAY99 explicitly specifies BEP20 only, while Kunavo specifies USDT/TRC20 or USDC/Base only.[[aipay99](https://aipay99.com/en/help)][[kunavo](https://kunavo.com/guides/pay-for-ai-api-with-crypto)]

My recommendation is to keep these as supplier leads until verification is complete. Do not preload a large reseller balance, promise uninterrupted access, or collect customer money for restricted services on the strength of a supplier’s marketing page. At present, the evidence supports a sourcing shortlist—not a reliable, authorized wholesale supply chain.
